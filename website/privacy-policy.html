Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 18, 2026

HerdTrackr ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and farm/organization details when you register
  • Livestock Data: Animal records including identification numbers, health records, weight data, breeding information, treatment records, and photos you upload
  • Farm Data: Pasture information, location data, farm operations records
  • Payment Information: Billing details for subscription plans (processed securely by third-party payment providers)

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: App features used, interaction patterns, performance data
  • Location Data: GPS coordinates when you use location-based features (with your permission)
  • Camera & Photos: Photos and camera access for ear tag scanning and animal photos (with your permission)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

  • Providing and maintaining the HerdTrackr service
  • Processing your livestock management data and generating reports
  • Syncing your data across devices via cloud storage
  • Processing AI-powered OCR for ear tag recognition
  • Billing and subscription management
  • Customer support and responding to inquiries
  • Improving our app features and user experience
  • Sending important service updates and notifications
  • Compliance with legal obligations and regulatory requirements

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Data Storage

Your data is stored:

  • Locally: On your device for offline access and functionality
  • Cloud Storage: Encrypted backups using Supabase infrastructure with bank-level security
  • Location: Data centers with industry-standard security certifications

3.2 Security Measures

  • End-to-end encryption for data transmission
  • Secure authentication protocols
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Access controls and role-based permissions
  • Regular automated backups with redundancy

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We DO NOT sell your personal information or livestock data to third parties. We may share your information only in these limited circumstances:

  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly authorize data sharing
  • Service Providers: Third-party services that help us operate (cloud hosting, payment processing, analytics) under strict confidentiality agreements
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or government regulations
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (users will be notified)
  • Team Members: Users you add to your farm organization can access shared data based on permissions you set

5. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and data - Submit a deletion request
  • Export: Download your livestock data in portable formats (PDF, CSV)
  • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications
  • Restrict Processing: Limit how we process your data
  • Data Portability: Transfer your data to another service

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@herdtrackr.co.za or visit our Data Deletion Request page

6. Camera and Location Permissions

6.1 Camera Access

We request camera permission for:

  • AI-powered OCR scanning of ear tags
  • Taking photos of livestock for records
  • Documenting health conditions or farm operations

Camera access is only used when you actively use these features. Images are stored locally on your device and optionally synced to your cloud account. We do not access your camera without your explicit action.

6.2 Location Access

We request location permission for:

  • GPS-tagging pasture locations and animal movements
  • Tracking livestock location history
  • Compliance and audit trail purposes

Location tracking only occurs when you use location-based features. You can disable location access in your device settings at any time.

7. Children's Privacy

HerdTrackr is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we discover that a child under 18 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data:

  • Active Accounts: As long as your account is active
  • After Deletion: Up to 90 days for backup and recovery purposes
  • Legal Requirements: Longer if required by law or regulatory compliance
  • Aggregated Data: Anonymous analytics data may be retained indefinitely

9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred and processed in countries outside South Africa. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your information in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

10. Third-Party Services

Our app uses third-party services:

  • Supabase: Cloud database and authentication
  • Payment Processors: Secure payment handling
  • Analytics Services: App performance and usage analytics

These services have their own privacy policies and data handling practices.

11. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies for:

  • Authentication and session management
  • Remembering your preferences
  • Analytics and performance monitoring
  • Security and fraud prevention

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes via:

  • Email notification to your registered address
  • In-app notification
  • Updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy

Continued use of HerdTrackr after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

13. Compliance

HerdTrackr complies with:

  • Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) - South Africa
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) - European Union (where applicable)
  • Industry best practices for data security and privacy
