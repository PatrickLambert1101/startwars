Data Deletion Request

We respect your right to control your personal data. If you wish to delete your HerdTrackr account and all associated data, you can do so using any of the methods below.

⚠️ Important: This Action is Permanent

Deleting your account will permanently remove:

  • Your account and login credentials
  • All livestock records (animals, health records, weight data, breeding records)
  • All photos and attachments
  • Pasture and movement data
  • Treatment protocols and reports
  • Team member associations
  • All other data associated with your account

This action cannot be undone. We recommend exporting your data before requesting deletion.

How to Delete Your Data

Option 1: Delete From Within the App (Recommended)

✅ Fastest Method

You can delete your account directly from the HerdTrackr app:

  1. Open the HerdTrackr app
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Scroll down to Account Management
  4. Tap Delete Account
  5. Confirm your decision

Processing time: Immediate. Your account will be deleted instantly.

What Happens Next?

Processing Timeline

  • In-app deletion: Immediate (instant deletion)
  • Email/Phone request: Within 30 days of receiving your request
  • Form submission: Within 30 days of receiving your form

Verification Process

For security reasons, we may need to verify your identity before processing deletion requests submitted via email or phone. We'll contact you using the email address or phone number associated with your account.

Data Retention

After deletion:

  • Immediate removal: Your data will be removed from active systems immediately
  • Backup retention: Data may remain in encrypted backups for up to 90 days
  • Legal obligations: Some data may be retained if required by law (e.g., tax records, transaction history)
  • Anonymized data: Aggregated, anonymized analytics data may be retained for product improvement

Before You Delete

Export Your Data

We recommend exporting your livestock data before deletion:

  1. Open the HerdTrackr app
  2. Go to Reports
  3. Tap Export All Data
  4. Choose format: PDF or CSV
  5. Save to your device or email to yourself

Cancel Subscription

If you have an active subscription, please cancel it before deleting your account:

  • iOS: Settings > Your Name > Subscriptions > HerdTrackr > Cancel
  • Android: Play Store > Menu > Subscriptions > HerdTrackr > Cancel

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel your subscription.

Your Rights

Under South African data protection law (POPIA) and international regulations (GDPR where applicable), you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Object to processing of your data
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator

For more information about your privacy rights, see our Privacy Policy.
